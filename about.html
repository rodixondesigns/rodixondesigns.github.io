Professional Journey

I'm an expert in User Experience Design with more than a decade and a half of experience navigating both corporate environments and consulting engagements. My path to design wasn't traditional (I majored in English Lit). I fell in love with UX and design through a bit of luck, curiosity, and a knack for untangling complex problems.

How am I different?

I've operated successfully in both worlds: the corporate in-house environment AND the fast-paced consulting arena. This dual perspective means I understand not just how to craft great user experiences, but how to navigate stakeholder dynamics, tight timelines, and the very human complexities that come with real-world product development.

My Design Philosophy

Great design isn't just about aesthetics or even usability—it's about cutting through complexity to solve genuine human problems. I approach every project with:
  • Strategic clarity over design theater
  • Practical solutions that work within business constraints
  • Research-driven insights that inform smart design decisions
  • Clear communication that gets stakeholders aligned
I treat every project like solving a puzzle: What's the real problem here? What assumptions are we making? What do users actually need versus what they say they want? Then I design solutions that work in the messy reality of business, not just in design theory.

What I value...

  • 🔍 Curiosity over assumptions
    I ask "why" a lot. Like, a lot a lot. Whether it's understanding user behavior or questioning business requirements, I've learned that the best solutions come from really understanding the problem first.

  • 🤝 Humans before metrics
    Yes, I care about conversion rates and retention. But I care more about the person on the other side of the screen having a genuinely good experience. Good business metrics usually follow good human experiences.

  • 🛠️ Systems that outlast me
    I don't just solve today's problem—I try to leave behind frameworks, processes, and tools that help teams keep solving problems after I'm gone. Sustainable design isn't just about the environment.
  • 🌱 Growing people, not just products
    Some of my proudest moments are watching designers I've mentored land amazing jobs or solve problems in ways I never would have thought of. Talent multiplication is real.

  • 🦸‍♀️ UX thinking as superpower
    UX expertise isn't just about wireframes—it's a way of seeing the world. I spot connections others miss, understand what drives business value, and turn messy constraints into elegant solutions. It's pattern recognition for human problems and product opportunities.

  • 🏠 Leaving places better
    Whether it's an organization, a product, or a team, I try to leave things a little better than I found them. Not in a savior complex way, but in a "let's make this more sustainable and humane" way.
